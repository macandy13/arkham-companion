Change chaos bag tokens where they're shown

3 stops1 question241 of 583 changed lines on the tour

The problem

Changing the chaos bag by hand took a row of controls that showed all the time, though a card asks for it only now and then: two steppers for bless and curse, a group to pick how long a change lasts and an Add a token button, each in a look of its own.

What changes

Every token lies on the tray as before. A tap on one rings it in brass and opens the app's menu under the tray: seal it on an investigator, or remove it (a bless or curse token goes back to its pool; in a campaign game a removal is for the scenario or the campaign). The tray ends on a dashed token with a +, whose menu holds every kind of token to add and, in a campaign game, how long the change lasts. A bless or curse token comes from its pool and is off while the pool is empty. The menus close on a tap outside or on Escape, which no longer closes the dialog with them.

Question for you

  1. Should a token's menu open under the tray, from its start, rather than right under the token?

    Concerns stop 2. Picked: Under the tray: lined up with the token, a menu from a token near either edge ran off the dialog..

One way to close a menu

useDismiss closes a menu on a press outside or on Escape, shared by the split pills, the connections line and the chaos bag.
