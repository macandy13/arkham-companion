One pill per action with its other ways in a menu

3 stops1 question84 of 868 changed lines on the tour

The problem

Since investigators can fight, evade and investigate by a card effect, the details showed one button per way: fight as an action, fight without one, and fight by card effect for each investigator. With two investigators that was three fight and three evade buttons, so the row grew long and the usual action was hard to find.

What changes

Each action is now one pill. A tap takes it the usual way, as the turn investigator's action with its cost arrows. A ⋮ beside it opens a menu of every way, the action first, then without an action, then by card effect for each investigator in play. The rare ways stay one step away.

Question for you

  1. Outside an investigator's turn there is no usual way, so the pill itself opens the menu. Fine?

1UI · details action row

The split pill

SplitAction shows the primary way as the action button with its cost arrows and a ⋮ that opens the menu, primary first. Without a primary the pill opens the menu; without other ways it is a plain button. The menu closes on a pick, Escape or a press elsewhere.
