Escape the Dorms advances on its own, and the lead hands Dr. Armitage to an investigator

What changes

Escape the Dorms advances once each investigator still in the scenario is at Miskatonic Quad, whether the last one walks in or the last one away is defeated or resigns. Searching for Dr. Armitage puts Dr. Henry Armitage under the control of the investigator the lead picks.

1

Each investigator at a location

An objective checked as an investigator enters the location and as one is eliminated, on the condition that someone is still in the scenario and nobody is elsewhere. With nobody left, even after one effect eliminates everyone (Wild Flames), the scenario ends with no resolution instead.
