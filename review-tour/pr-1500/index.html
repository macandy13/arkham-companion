MoveEnemy takes a destination rule toward a location

3 stops0 questions62 of 274 changed lines on the tour

The problem

Each kind of enemy movement had its own command: a card's "moves 1 location towards X" (MoveEnemyTowards), a hunter's step, the elusive retreat. Each found its next location and asked about ties in its own way, so the next movement keyword (patrol) would have needed a fourth.

What changes

MoveEnemy takes a destination rule: straight to a location as before, or one step toward a location. One step finder gives the first steps of the shortest ways, and equally short ones become one decision (move:<enemy>), answered by the same move with to naming the step. This slice brings "towards a location"; hunters and the retreat follow (ME.13b, ME.13c).

    Checked by packages/engine/src/enemies.test.ts › MoveEnemy toward a location › lets the lead investigator pick between two equally short ways

  • An answer naming a location off its shortest ways is refused.

    Checked by packages/engine/src/enemies.test.ts › MoveEnemy toward a location › rejects a chosen step that is not the first of its shortest ways

  • Cards that move enemies towards a location play as before (Ghouls towards the Parlor).
