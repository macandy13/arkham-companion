The Smoke and Mirrors suspects' reactions

One new window event, 'succeeded' (after any passed test, or one basic action's), heard by the cards at the test's location through a new 'here' scope, with an optional margin condition. Together with a successful-evade variant of 'evaded', it covers David Renfield, Margaret Liu and Cornelia Akely. Reading a codex entry is a deferred command, like the player-card ones.
