The ArkhamDB check owns card pictures and keywords

1 stop0 questions41 of 299 changed lines on the tour

The problem

The six scenarios' cards.test.ts each had tables of picture paths, keyword lists and spawn locations for a few hand-picked cards, restating card data that ArkhamDB has for every card. The test owners rule says printed data belongs to the ArkhamDB check, which compared stats but not these.

What changes

arkhamdb_check.test.ts now also compares each hand-entered card's keywords, with their details (spawn: Attic), and its front and back picture paths with ArkhamDB's normalised card. Keyword lines compare ignoring case, Elite stays out (ArkhamDB's parser adds it from the trait the check already compares), a back picture is held to ArkhamDB only where ArkhamDB names one, and a short list names the keywords left unchecked with their reasons. The scenarios drop their picture, keyword and spawn-location tables; The Masked Hunter keeps one row, since the check skips it as the agenda's linked side.
