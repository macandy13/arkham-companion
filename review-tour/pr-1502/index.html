Elokoss cannot move or make attacks of opportunity

4 stops0 questions52 of 222 changed lines on the tour

What changes

Elokoss, the Queen of Ash boss, cannot move and cannot make attacks of opportunity, which the players had to apply by hand. Two new enemy restrictions carry this: an enemy that cannot move counts as closed out of every location, and one that cannot make attacks of opportunity raises none.

1

Two new enemy restrictions

EnemyRestriction gains move and attackOfOpportunity, next to Handcuffs' ready and placeDoom.
