Swap places with an enemy, as Ethereal Slip does

3 stops1 question50 of 360 changed lines on the tour

The problem

A player who plays Ethereal Slip trades places with an enemy, and the app had no way to do it: dragging the pawn and then the enemy made the enemy engage the investigator, or follow them, on the way.

What changes

One engine command swaps an investigator and an enemy. The enemy is put at the investigator's location first, not engaging yet; the investigator then moves to the enemy's location as any card effect moves them (paying an enter cost, revealing it, after-you-enter effects), taking the enemies engaged with them along; only then does the swapped enemy engage whoever is left where it now stands. Where a card keeps the enemy out of the investigator's location, as Barricade does, the whole swap is refused. The enemy details offer it as a Swap pill in the Adjust group, whose menu names each investigator at another location, and offer none for an Elite enemy; how far away the enemy may be is the players' call.

Question for you

  1. Where Barricade keeps the enemy out, should the swap be refused rather than move only the investigator?

    Concerns stop 1. Picked: Refused: "cannot" is absolute, and nothing in the rules says to do half a swap..
