Put the chaos bag at the header's left end, and the bag and token pool in the phone's compact header

3 stops0 questions92 of 243 changed lines on the tour

The problem

The bag stood above the victory display, in the middle of the playmat's labels, and the phone's compact header had no room for the bag or the token pool: the players had to expand the header to find the bag and could not reach the pool at all.

What changes

The bag moves to the header's left end, a place of its own that mirrors the pool at the right end (top left on a wide header). On a phone the compact header keeps both as small placeholders at either end, its four cards a little smaller to make room. The bag opens its dialog from there; a tap on the pool expands the header on a new page for the pool, where its tokens are large enough to drag.
