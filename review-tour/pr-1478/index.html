Add any chaos token or remove the one tapped, for the scenario or the campaign

3 stops0 questions62 of 207 changed lines on the tour

The problem

A card or a resolution says "add a Cultist token to the chaos bag for the rest of the campaign" or "remove a Skull token", and the chaos bag dialog could only seal tokens and change bless and curse.

What changes

The token the players tap on the tray gets a Remove button beside its Seal buttons. Below the bless and curse steppers, a Change the bag group opens a row of every other kind of token to add. In a campaign game two pills pick whether a change lasts for this scenario or the rest of the campaign; the resolution then writes a campaign change into the log, as it does for a card's change. A standalone game has only the scenario.
