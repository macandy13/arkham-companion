One way to change the game from the screen

5 stops0 questions142 of 1429 changed lines on the tour

The problem

The play screen's GameActions had about 50 methods. Most were one-line wrappers around a single engine command, and several concepts had two or three names: evade and evadeEnemy, moveTo and moveByCard, testByCard next to investigate and fight, and four doom methods. Fixes took three routes (ManualAdjust, a real command, AdjustInvestigator), so whether a tap fired forced effects depended on which method a component happened to call.

What changes

GameActions is down to 19 methods. Move, investigate, fight, evade and engage take one How: as an action, as a free action, or { byCard: investigator } (Shortcut, In the Know, Marksmanship, Heroic Rescue). An effect the players carry out from card text is perform(...effects), a fix is correct(...adjustments). Components build what they perform with the same factories card data uses, which now take instance IDs too: actions.perform(dealDamageTo(enemy.id, 1)), never a command literal.
