Card actions that cost two actions

3 stops1 question38 of 274 changed lines on the tour

What changes

A two-action card ability used to count off one action. A card action now says how many actions it costs, and the offer passes that number on to the play screen, which counts that many off and draws that many arrows.

Question for you

  1. Southside's two-action 'draw a combined total of 3 cards, divided as they choose' stays by hand, because one deferred draw for the taker would misstate it. Is that fine?

    Concerns stop 3.
