Show and change a location's connections in its details

2 stops1 question48 of 304 changed lines on the tour

The problem

A card says "the River Docks are connected to the Unvisited Isle" or "the Parlor is no longer connected to the Hallway", and the players had no way to tell the app, so moves, hunters and "nearest" kept going by the old map.

What changes

Under who is there, the location details name where a move from it goes, as one line. A tap on the line opens a menu in the app's menu look with a tick per other location in play; a tap connects the two both ways on this location's map, or cuts their connection, only one way when only this location leads there. This is the first piece of a shared rule for changing the board by hand: the thing is shown, and you change it where it's shown.

Question for you

  1. When both locations lead to each other, should a tap cut only the way from the opened location, rather than both ways?

    Concerns stop 1. Picked: Both ways, since a card's "no longer connected" leaves no move between them..
