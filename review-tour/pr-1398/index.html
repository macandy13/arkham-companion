Fight, evade and investigate by a card effect

A "By card effect" group in the location and enemy details starts an investigation, fight or evade for any active investigator, out of turn and from another location, as cards like In the Know, Counterpunch and Marksmanship allow.

1API

One action for the three tests

GameActions.testByCard plays the engine's Investigate with locationId, or Fight and Evade with atAnyLocation. It spends none of the turn's actions; the test stays open as a decision.

The group picks who tests

Testers are the active investigators on the map. With one, a tap starts the test; with more, the group turns into a who-tests picker with Cancel.
