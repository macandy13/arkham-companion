The play screen hands actions and busy down through one context

2 stops0 questions132 of 2145 changed lines on the tour

The problem

Nearly every part of the play screen took the open game's actions and whether a step was busy as two props, only to pass them further down: 24 components took actions, 25 busy, and the header built a props bag with both for its piles. Agenda and act took the whole store just to reach the actions.

What changes

The play screen puts the open game's actions and busy into one context, PlayProvider, and each part that plays a step reads them with usePlay. Everything else, the board included, stays an explicit prop, and the header takes onSpawn instead of the store. Tests wrap a part drawn alone in one provider, PlayingOn, which plays on the table the test built.
