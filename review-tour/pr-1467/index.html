A group clue cost to enter a location

3 stops1 question80 of 621 changed lines on the tour

What changes

Underground Cistern and Sluice Control cost clues per investigator, spent as a group, to enter while unrevealed. A location can now carry a cost to enter, and the move pays it like any group clue cost: the players split it, and the move can't be made while the group holds too few clues.

Question for you

  1. A move by a card effect picked on the map (Shortcut) pays with the engine's split, the mover first, without asking who pays. Asking there too would need the map's pick to open the payment dialog; is it fine to leave that to #1423?

    Concerns stop 3.

A location's cost to enter

enterCost is the clues the group spends to enter a location, only while it is unrevealed, since the cost is printed on that side. enterCostPayment turns the players' split (or the engine's) into the clue spends, and refuses a move the group can't pay.

The move pill asks who pays

BoardView.enterCost and canEnter tell the screen the cost and whether the group can pay it. The move pill asks who pays first, as a card action's group cost does, and is off while the group can't pay.
