Fan attached cards over the details page's bottom edge

3 stops1 question36 of 358 changed lines on the tour

The problem

The details of an enemy or a location listed its attached cards (Handcuffs, Barricade, Locked Door) as a row of pills, each with a × to discard it. The row read as one more set of buttons between the values and the actions, and a stray tap on the × discarded a card at once.

What changes

The attached cards are now small cards fanned over the bottom edge of the page, half outside it, with a dashed card at the end to attach another. A tap opens the card large, and Discard sits under it there. On a phone, where the sheet's bottom is the screen's edge, the fan sits over the sheet's top edge at the right instead.

Question for you

  1. Discard is now one step further away, under the card's large view, instead of a × in the row. Fine?

1UI · attached cards

The fan and its Discard

Each attached card is a small card button showing its picture. Its large view (CardZoom, which now takes children) has Discard under it. In a card dialog the fan goes through a portal into the dialog's tucked slot, since the scrolling page would cut off what reaches past its edge.

3UI · card dialog styles

Placing it on the edge

CSS anchor positioning centres the slot on the page's bottom edge, with a fallback for browsers without it. The page keeps room above the edge for the half that lies on it. A phone moves the fan to the sheet's top edge.
