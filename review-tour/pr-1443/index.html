One CardFilter for every card description

3 stops0 questions49 of 149 changed lines on the tour

The problem

Five types described what is printed on a card: CardFilter for references, EncounterMatch for a matched encounter draw, and the printed half of EnemyFilter and LocationFilter, each with its own subset of code, name, trait and type and its own matcher. A card asking for "a non-Elite location" or "the first card named X" had to find which of them could say it.

What changes

CardFilter (code, name, trait, without a trait, type, unique) is now the one description of a card's print, with one matcher. A matched draw takes it as its match, and the enemy and location filters extend it with only what the table adds (unengaged, ready, no doom; connecting). Picking among candidates follows in ME.18b to 18d.
