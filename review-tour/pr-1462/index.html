feat(engine) · MC2.4b · as of f7ca1b2

An extra action to enter, leave or investigate a location

3 stops1 question90 of 365 changed lines on the tour

What changes

Waterfront District makes investigating it cost an extra action, and Arcane Lock makes entering or leaving its location cost one. An action cost modifier can now name a location, and the engine adds it whenever a move leaves or enters that location, or an investigation happens there.

Question for you

  1. Moves and investigations made by card abilities (Merchant District's free move, an '[action]: Investigate' ability) don't add the location's cost yet; the players count it off at the table until #1423. Is that fine for now?

    Concerns stop 1.

A cost modifier that names a location

LocationActionCostModifier names the card itself ('self', Waterfront District) or the card a treachery is attached to ('host', Arcane Lock), and the actions done with it: enter, leave or investigate.
