Hunters move with the prey destination rule

4 stops0 questions44 of 633 changed lines on the tour

The problem

A hunter's step had its own commands (MoveHunter, plus the way-finding inside MoveHunters) and its own tie decisions, apart from the card effect that moves an enemy towards a location. After the players picked whom a hunter hunts, that separate path also forgot that a Barricaded location is closed to it.

What changes

A hunter's step is now MoveEnemy { toward: 'prey' }, the hunter's destination rule next to "toward a location". enemyWay gives both rules' first steps; the prey rule can first leave a choice between equally near investigators, answered by a move toward the chosen one's location. MoveHunters only picks the hunters and asks their order.
