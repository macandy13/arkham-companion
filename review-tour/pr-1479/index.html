Acts declare how they advance

4 stops0 questions68 of 384 changed lines on the tour

The problem

How an act advances was never stated on the card: the board view and the text board found it by matching the shape of the act's abilities (a valueOf on its own clues, a roundEnds ability with a groupClues condition, a group clue cost with an advance). An act written any other way silently lost its clue counter on the board.

What changes

An act now declares how it advances (CardStats.advance): a clue threshold spent as a group, a round-end spend at a location, or an objective on its own clues. The act builders compile the declaration into the action or ability that does it (withAdvance), and the board reads the declaration itself.
