Lay the chaos bag out as a token tray with In the bag, Sealed and Reveal tabs

2 stops0 questions68 of 574 changed lines on the tour

The problem

The chaos bag's own dialog showed everything on one page: the contents as count pills, the bless and curse steppers, Reveal tokens and the sealed tokens. On a phone that page scrolled, and it was about to grow with adding and removing tokens (ME.29b).

What changes

The dialog becomes the tray from the chosen design, with three tabs. In the bag shows every token one by one on a felt tray, as if tipped out on the table, and a tap picks one to seal. Sealed lists what is sealed, with Release. Reveal steps the count, lays the revealed tokens on the tray and ends on Put back and Reveal, the view's one brass primary.

Every token on the tray

The counts expand to one entry per token, so two −1s are two tokens. A pick is a kind and which of its tokens it is, so a step taken elsewhere, such as Undo, can only drop the pick, never move it to another token.
