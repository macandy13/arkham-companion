feat(engine) · MC2.12c · as of 82570bd

Languor forbids a card type until the round ends

4 stops1 question148 of 513 changed lines on the tour

What changes

Languor makes its holder discard the top card of their deck as each turn begins, and then they cannot play or commit cards of that type this round. The app has no player cards, so it asks what the card was and keeps the type as a restriction until the round ends.

Question for you

  1. Lasting restrictions sit in GameMeta next to the round's other bookkeeping, cleared by RoundEnded. Lasting modifiers (ME.7d #869) could later share this state; is keeping them separate for now fine?

    Concerns stop 1.

A restriction that lasts until the round ends

An investigator restriction without a card scope (InvestigatorRestriction) can be given to one investigator until the round ends (LastingRestriction). cards gains the verb commit, and its filter can name skills.
