Prey as selectors over the investigators, read at card load

3 stops0 questions60 of 546 changed lines on the tour

The problem

Prey texts were read from their text each time an enemy engaged, hunted or spawned, by a reader of its own that built small comparison functions ("Highest [combat]", "Nearest to another [[Cultist]] enemy", "Bearer only"). Spawn and card effects now pick with one selector; prey was the last reader of its own.

What changes

The prey text is read once, when the card data loads, into CardStats.preyOn: a Selector among the investigators and whether it says "only". preferredByPrey resolves it with the same resolver, among the investigators in question, so a prey's comparison is a selector measure like any other.

1engine

Each prey text is a selector

An extreme word with a counter or skill becomes a stat measure, "nearest to another X enemy" a distanceToOther measure, and naming texts filters on the investigator reference (a name, the trait of their location, the bearer). Anything else names an investigator.

Two new measures

stat reads a counter, remaining health or sanity, or a skill with its boosts and modifiers (investigatorMeasure); distanceToOther counts from the investigator to the nearest other enemy the reference names, infinite with none.
