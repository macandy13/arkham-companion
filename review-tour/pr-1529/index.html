Keep the daily digest when main deploys to gh-pages

1 stop0 questions1 of 6 changed lines on the tour

The problem

Every push to main redeploys the app to gh-pages and deletes folders it doesn't know about, so the digest published at 05:00 was gone after the next merge.

What changes

The deploy keeps digest/ the same way it already keeps pr-preview/ and review-tour/.

1Change

The deploy leaves the digest alone

digest/ joins the clean-exclude list, next to the previews and review tours.

.github/workflows/deploy.yml+4 −2Open in diff
48           clean-exclude: |49             pr-preview/50             review-tour/51+            digest/

How we know it works

  • The deploy deleted the digest: gh-pages had digest/ after the 16:33 digest run, and the main deploy at 16:37 removed it.

    git log origin/gh-pages: c0072b46 Digest for 2026-11-27, then fb7878f3 Deploying to gh-pages

That's the whole change.
