The elusive retreat moves away from investigators

2 stops1 question49 of 177 changed lines on the tour

The problem

The elusive retreat (RetreatEnemy) was the last enemy move with its own way-finding and its own tie decision (retreat:<enemy>), after a card's move towards a location and the hunter's step moved onto MoveEnemy.

What changes

The retreat is now MoveEnemy { toward: 'awayFromInvestigators' }: a connecting location without investigators if there is one, else any open connecting location. RetreatEnemy only disengages and exhausts the enemy, then takes that step, so the players' choice is the shared move:<enemy> decision. That completes ME.13: one move command, three destination rules.

Question for you

  1. The rules print "disengages, moves …, and exhausts". The engine exhausts before the step, so the enemy engages nobody where it arrives, as it already did before this PR. Is that the reading to keep (#1518), or should it engage an investigator there first?

    Concerns stop 2. Picked: Keep it: nothing changes, and #1518 stays open for a ruling.
