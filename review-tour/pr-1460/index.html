Spawn instructions as selectors, read at card load

3 stops0 questions36 of 495 changed lines on the tour

The problem

Spawn instructions were read from their text each time an enemy spawned, by a reader of its own: string matching for "Nearest empty location", "Location with the most clues" or "A or B (whichever is nearest to you)", with its own nearest, farthest and most-clues helpers. Card effects already pick among candidates with the selector from ME.18b, so spawning had a second way of saying the same thing.

What changes

The text is read once, when the card data loads, into CardStats.spawnAt: alternatives of "Engaged with prey" or a Selector among the locations in play. Spawning tries them in turn with the selector's resolver, so the nearest, the farthest and the most clues are the same measures a card effect uses.

Each instruction is a selector

The fixed forms map to selectors among the locations in play: empty, a trait, connectedTo your location, the nearest or farthest, the most clues. "(empty, if able)" is two alternatives, the empty locations first, and anything else names a location.

Spawning resolves them like a card effect

candidatesOf lists a selector's locations, counted from whoever drew the enemy. Text naming no location in the game, or "connected to yours" with nobody who drew it, still becomes a choice among all locations, never a guess.
