Spreading Flames' Parley and agenda tests resolve in the app

2 stops1 question19 of 194 changed lines on the tour

What changes

Each Spreading Flames agenda offers a Parley: test intellect (2), and on a success discard a Bystander enemy at your location. As Past Curfew and Lit Up advance, each investigator in player order tests willpower (3) or agility (3), and each who fails takes 1 horror or 1 damage.

Question for you

  1. The Parley is offered only with a Bystander at your location, as the task planned. No official ruling says whether the test alone counts as changing the game state, so the rules page marks this as our reading. Should the Parley stay hidden without a Bystander, or always be offered?

    Concerns stop 1.

A Parley only with a Bystander there

The Parley's condition counts the Bystanders at the taker's location; a success discards one, picked when several are there. Discarding is no defeat, so Doomed places no doom.

Each investigator tests as the agenda advances

All three agendas carry the Parley. Past Curfew and Lit Up repeat their test for each investigator, the lead first, with each outcome for its tester; Lit Up places its set-aside Fire! first.
