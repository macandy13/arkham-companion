Enemies at a location with Fire! attached cannot be damaged

3 stops0 questions39 of 162 changed lines on the tour

What changes

In Queen of Ash, the first two agendas say each enemy at a location with Fire! attached cannot be damaged. An enemy restriction can now hold only an enemy at a location with a matching card attached, and damage dealt to such an enemy does nothing.

1

A restriction for enemies at a burning location

EnemyCardRestriction gains damaged, the scope 'eachEnemy' and atLocationWith: the enemy is held only while a matching card is attached to its location, an engaged enemy counting at its investigator's.
