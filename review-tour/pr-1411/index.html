Script Ashen Rebirth; choice options offered while a condition holds

3 stops0 questions53 of 291 changed lines on the tour

What changes

Problem: a choice like "take damage or horror, whichever you have more of" offers only one answer, decided by the table, so the app left it to the players. Change: a choice option may carry a condition (when), checked as the choice is raised, and a new condition atLeast compares two values. An option alone applies without asking.

1engine

Options offered while their condition holds

Before the references become IDs, the choice drops the options whose when fails. One option left applies without asking; none left does nothing. Because a test's outcomes resolve after the answer, the condition reads the table after the test.

Comparing two values

atLeast(value, than) holds when one value on the table is at least another, each summed over what it names. On a tie both options hold, so the drawer picks.
