In-game dialogs

Every in-game dialog in the design language, on a tablet and on a phone. Card pictures load from ArkhamDB; the app draws its own card faces. The rules are in docs/product.md, "Design language".

Investigate

A full skill test: the tested skill, the modifier for committed cards, the token and the calculation. Passed carries the clues discovered, 1 unless a card says more.

Fight, evade

Same test block. Damage dealt is a number in the Passed row: 1 for a basic fight, raised for a weapon (here +1 from a .45 Automatic). Evade has no number.

Attack of opportunity

Damage and horror come from the enemy card and sit in the row as numbers, so a soak or a card effect is changed before Done.

Encounter card test

The treachery is the card and its revelation runs the same test block. A fail that offers a choice shows it under the row, with its number.

Other decisions

Spawn, Prey and card choices show the card whose text asks. Plain rows answer on tap, so there is no Done. When the choice is a location, Pick on the map lowers the dialog and lights up the allowed locations; tapping one answers.

Skill test

Opened from a skill on the investigator panel: the investigator shows as a round portrait, not a card. A calculator, so no footer; tap a second skill to add it.

Clue payment

The act lies on top on a tablet and peeks out on the left on a phone; payers are rows with their faces and clue tokens to tap.

Enemy details

Card left, stats and actions on the page. One row of actions: each action icon is one action it costs (Fight shows two because Frozen in Fear makes the first fight cost 1 more), and free ones carry the free-ability icon from the game. Fight turns this page into the fight test above while the card stays put.

Location details

Same spread as the enemy. Clues read left / printed like enemy health (the Hallway prints none; the Attic would show 2 / 2 and then 1 / 2). On phones every dialog, card dialogs included, is the same sheet with the card peeking out on the left.

Card zoom

Treacheries and player cards in play: the card beside its owner and its actions. Test to discard opens the test block beside the same card.

Agenda and act

Landscape cards lie on top. Advance is the one primary.

Scenario reference

The reference card is upright, so it lies beside the page with its piles.

Encounter piles

No subject card: the cards are the content, so it stays a wide page.

Game log

Not tied to a moment in the game and can grow long, so it is a page of its own rather than a dialog: the full height of the tablet at the wide width, the whole phone screen, scrolling inside. × returns to the table.

Question

Narrow page on a tablet, a full-width sheet on a phone with both buttons sharing the width. No ×. A question before deleting something has a red primary instead.
