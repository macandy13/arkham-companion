Phone play screen

A clickable prototype to discuss how the play screen works on a phone held upright. The Gathering, round 2, Roland's turn. Everything is clickable; nothing is saved.

Overview

A compact row of header cards (encounter deck, agenda, act, scenario), the round and phase row, the map shrunk to fit the space left, and the investigator whose turn it is. Tap a header card or the investigator to open its details. Tap a location for its sheet; the expand button (or tapping an empty part of the map) shows the map full screen.

Details

Two rows: the scenario cards on top, the investigators below. Swipe up and down to switch between the rows, left and right to switch cards within a row. With a mouse: wheel or the arrows at the edges, or ↑ ↓ ← →. Esc or the X goes back to the overview.

Full-screen map

Pinch or the +/− buttons zoom, dragging pans. Tap a location to move there or investigate. The turn investigator stays docked at the bottom; tap it for details.
