Phone play screen

A clickable prototype to discuss how the play screen works on a phone held upright. The Gathering, round 2, Roland's turn. Everything is clickable; nothing is saved.

Overview

A compact row of header cards (encounter deck, agenda, act, scenario), the round and phase row, the map shrunk to fit the space left, and the investigator whose turn it is. Tap a header card or the investigator to open it full screen. Tap a location for its sheet; the expand button (or tapping an empty part of the map) opens the map full screen.

Full screen

Three rows: the scenario cards, the map and the investigators. Swipe up and down to switch rows, left and right to switch cards within the scenario cards or the investigators. On the map, a vertical swipe only counts when it starts on the strip at the top or bottom edge; anywhere else dragging pans and pinching zooms the map. With a mouse: the strips, the wheel, or ↑ ↓ ← →. Esc or the X goes back to the overview.

The scenario cards row has the encounter deck leftmost but opens on the agenda. The investigator whose turn it is wears a brass Turn badge; the lead investigator a pentagon with a flashlight, like the game's token. Threat areas sit above the investigator, as small cards in the overview.

Location view

The third mode. Tapping a location (on the preview or the full map) replaces the map row with that location: its card, shroud and clues, the investigators and enemies there, and Investigate or Move for the investigator whose turn it is. Its connected locations sit around it in their map directions; tap one to jump there. The swipe rows stay the same (scenario cards above, investigators below); the Map button goes back to the whole map.
