# Play screen: investigators and enemies

## Investigator bar

The [investigator bar](../../apps/web/src/investigators/InvestigatorBar.tsx) shows each investigator's card drawn as the top of the printed landscape card, [`InvestigatorFace`](../../apps/web/src/investigators/InvestigatorFace.tsx): our own SVG drawing in scan units of the printed layout (`INVESTIGATOR_FACE`, measured on Roland Banks' scan, the exact card templates of MD.10) over the picture of the card's front (setup copies it onto the investigator as `Investigator.imageSrc`, since a resumed game has no investigator cards), lifted so the portrait's face shows, its printed frame and text under the face's own frame. The players hold the card itself, so its abilities and flavour stay off the play screen, the card is a short banner, and in the phone's compact view (the bar's `onOpen`) a slim strip (`INVESTIGATOR_STRIP`, `data-shape='strip'`: the art as a round portrait, the banner's plates and skill chain scaled side by side along the top, the counters on a paper strip under them, its places in tokens.css under `[data-card='investigator'][data-shape='strip']`). A tap on its art, or the strip's portrait, opens the whole card, [`InvestigatorCard`](../../apps/web/src/investigators/InvestigatorCard.tsx): the same face at the scan's full height (`full`, `data-shape='full'`) with the panel's skills and counters and the traits, ability and flavour texts on the notebook page (`PrintedInvestigator`, from the catalog), each ability its own paragraph (`cardParagraphs`) and a long text set smaller (`textLength`), in a dialog that fills the phone's screen like the card zoom. Its colours are the card theme's `--color-face-*` tokens, like the other faces. In the card pictures theme ([investigator_face.css](../../apps/web/src/investigators/investigator_face.css)) the banner and the whole card show the scan itself, unlifted, and our drawing hides: the banner keeps only its page strip, stretched over the scan's abilities, for the live counters, and the skill values show only when a modifier changes them; the whole card hides its notebook page and name, and its damage and horror counts sit on the printed heart and brain (`--investigator-*` in tokens.css, under `:root[data-card-theme='pictures']`). The strip keeps its drawing, since a scan cannot fold into it. The panel's HTML lies over the face's places, sized in its scan units (`--scan-unit`, `--investigator-*` in tokens.css), so roles and labels stay plain HTML:

- the class shield (colour and icon of the card's class) and their name and subname on the plates, the class and subname from the card catalog by the investigator's card code (`InvestigatorView.code`, `printedInvestigatorOf`),
- their four skills on the chain, each its value on its plate beside the skill's icon in the face's ink, showing the printed value with its lasting boost and the boost in a small badge (`InvestigatorView.boosts`, changed while adjusting with steppers in a row under the card, each boost after its skill's icon over its − and +, since two steppers are wider than a link on the chain, `GameActions.boostSkill`),
  - each opening a [skill test](../../apps/web/src/investigators/SkillTestDialog.tsx) on the card dialog shell with the investigator's round portrait over the page: the test block the decisions share ([decisions](play-screen-decisions.md)) with a difficulty stepper, the tokens drawn as round tokens (a symbol token showing its game symbol, its name kept for screen readers), calculated by `BoardView.skillTest` over the engine's `skillTestSuggestion` without changing the game,
- their counters on a strip of the spiral-bound notebook page, each on its token from the token set with the count in its disc ([`GameToken`](../../apps/web/src/tokens/GameToken.tsx), MD.8b): the clue token, health and sanity drawn as the card prints them (the shared heart and brain from [`cardFace.tsx`](../../apps/web/src/catalog/cardFace.tsx) with what is left on the symbol, in a ring of a pip per point of the printed maximum whose last pips, the damage or horror taken, are greyed ([`PipRing`](../../apps/web/src/investigators/PipRing.tsx)), one image each, named by what is left, "4 of 7"; on the whole card in the card pictures style they lie over the printed ones, a little larger to cover them), and the resource token; while the players adjust the board, the counters with their steppers leave the page for a row under the card,
- under the card, where they are and how their round goes, after a pentagon like the game's lead investigator token for `BoardView.lead`; the turn has no marker, as its controls' action arrows show it (on the map their pawns carry the pentagon and a glowing gilt ring),
- and the turn controls under the card, as the design language's buttons: the actions outlined pills, start turn and end the brass primary (`data-primary`) in the same pill as the status row's Next, a whole touch target tall and wide on a phone:
  - start turn,
  - actions left,
  - icon pills for investigate (off where a card forbids investigating their location, `LocationView.forbidden`: Locked Door), take a resource, draw and play (off while a card forbids them to play assets or events, `InvestigatorView.forbidden`: Dissonant Voices); play first opens its sheet on the card dialog shell, [`PlayCardDialog`](../../apps/web/src/investigators/PlayCardDialog.tsx), with their resources as tokens to tap like the clue payment's clues (one tap pays up to and with that token, `paysAfterTap`), and plays without paying or, the brass primary, pays what is tapped (`GameActions.playCard(cost)`, `PlayCard.cost`, one step); the × leaves without spending the action,
  - a button per card action the cards offer them (`BoardView.cardActions`, "Resign · Parlor"),
  - end.

Each investigator's panel carries their id as `data-investigator`, which the phone's detail view scrolls to and the map's token drag hit-tests for a drop ([`investigatorAt`](../../apps/web/src/investigators/dropTarget.ts)): a clue dragged off a location onto the panel is theirs ([map](play-screen-map.md)). The other way, the clue count is a drag source ([`useTokenDrag`](../../apps/web/src/screens/useTokenDrag.ts), the page's counterpart of the map's `usePieceDrag`, in screen points): a clue dragged off it onto the investigator's own location on the map (hit-tested by [`locationAt`](../../apps/web/src/map/dropTarget.ts), the tile's `data-location`) is placed there (`GameActions.placeClue`, `PlaceClues`), as a card or a failed test tells them to. A clue dropped on the act (hit-tested by [`overHeaderCard`](../../apps/web/src/screens/headerDropTarget.ts), the header place's `data-slot='act'`) does what the act's own button does ([`useClueDrag`](../../apps/web/src/investigators/useClueDrag.tsx)): an act the investigators advance by spending clues advances once they hold enough, after the players choose who pays (`useCluePayment`, `advanceAct`); on an act that takes clues on it, the investigator whose turn it is takes its action that costs a clue (`OfferedCardAction.clues`, Disrupting the Ritual), whose test decides whether a clue lands there. A drop elsewhere does nothing; the count is no drag source while they hold no clue, a step is queued, or the players adjust the board. A damage, horror or resource token dragged off the header's token pool ([`TokenPool`](../../apps/web/src/screens/TokenPool.tsx)) onto the panel is theirs as a card deals or gives it (`GameActions.adjust`, by rule, so damage can defeat them), one step a drop. Damage dropped on an enemy, on the map or on its card in a threat area (`ThreatArea`'s `data-enemy`, found by [`enemyAt`](../../apps/web/src/map/dropTarget.ts) before the panel under it), is dealt to the enemy (`damageEnemy`). The other way, damage or horror dragged off the panel's heart or brain, or a resource off its count, and dropped on the pool heals or spends one ([`useReturnToPool`](../../apps/web/src/screens/useReturnToPool.tsx), the same `adjust` with −1); a drop anywhere else keeps it. Nothing drags off the panel while the players adjust the board, since the steppers do it then.

## Threat area and play area

Their [threat area](../../apps/web/src/investigators/ThreatArea.tsx) is a row of small cards on the top edge of their card:

- treacheries
- and engaged enemies, the enemies with their health left (`EnemyView.health`, scaled by the player count through `enemyHealth`).

Each is a small card face in the card theme's colours (`SmallCard`, frame in its card type's colour): an art strip over a name plate, the enemies' health left on the art's corner. The strip shows the card's picture (`SmallCardArt`, an `img` at the card's width enlarged by `--small-card-art-zoom`), slid up to where the type prints its art (`--small-card-*-art-y` in tokens.css, fractions of the scan's height), with the art pattern until it loads; in the card pictures theme the whole scan is the card and the plate gives way to its printed name.

The threat area shows four cards (`THREAT_CARDS_SHOWN`); more scroll by whole cards with a swipe or its round arrows (CSS scroll snap). The row and its arrows are the shared [`CardStrip`](../../apps/web/src/screens/CardStrip.tsx), which the encounter dialog's pile uses too.

A tap on a treachery opens its details on the card shell ([`CardDetails`](../../apps/web/src/investigators/CardDetails.tsx), `DetailsDialog`; `BoardView.cardsIn` a zone gives id, name, type, traits and the pictures of both sides):

- the card beside the page, a treachery's front drawn as its card ([`TreacheryCard`](../../apps/web/src/treacheries/TreacheryCard.tsx), in scan units like the enemy card: `TREACHERY_FACE` and the frame's paths `TREACHERY_FRAME` in [`treacheryCardFrame.ts`](../../apps/web/src/treacheries/treacheryCardFrame.ts)): the art through the keyhole window cut from its picture (`TREACHERY_ART`), the bead chains and volutes, the set medallion, the name and traits on the page; in the card pictures theme the whole picture lies over it; any other card as its picture,
- whose threat area or play area it lies in as the kicker, with their portrait,
- and on the page the card actions it offers the investigator whose turn it is. Taking one keeps the dialog open and shows the decision it raises in its place (`useDecisionHost`), so a test to discard follows beside the same card; once the card leaves play its small card goes, and the dialog with it.

A tap on the shell's card opens the [card zoom](../../apps/web/src/screens/CardZoom.tsx), which only reads it: as big as a zoomed card, turned over with a button when it has a back, and without a picture its name, type and traits in our own frame.

The cards an investigator controls (their `playArea` zone, Lita Chantler after a Parley) lie under their card as the same small cards in the asset frame ([`PlayArea`](../../apps/web/src/investigators/PlayArea.tsx), `SmallCard`), each opening its details like a treachery.

## Enemy details

A tap on an enemy card opens its [details](../../apps/web/src/enemies/EnemyDetails.tsx) on the card shell (`DetailsDialog`, with the drawing as its `drawn` card in place of a picture): the engaged investigator in the kicker, the fight, the health left of its health for the player count ("3 / 5") and the evade as a `dl` on the page, and the action rows under them. Beside the page is the enemy drawn as its [card](../../apps/web/src/enemies/EnemyCard.tsx) (`BoardView.enemyStats`), in the Exact card theme our own SVG drawing of the printed enemy card, in scan units like the location's face (`ENEMY_FACE` and the frame's paths `ENEMY_FRAME` in [`enemyCardFrame.ts`](../../apps/web/src/enemies/enemyCardFrame.ts)):

- the name plate, with the frame's scrolls hanging from it (`OrnamentLine`, `scrollPath` in [`cardFace.tsx`](../../apps/web/src/catalog/cardFace.tsx)),
- fight, health and evade on chevrons, each one image named by its value ("Health 3 of 5"), the skill fight and evade test beside them, and the damage on the health as the shared `CountCircle`,
- traits, keywords and victory points on the lens of paper, in our own words as HTML in `foreignObject`s so they wrap,
- the attack as hearts and brains (`Heart`, `Brain` in `cardFace.tsx`) beside the set medallion,
- and the art cut from the picture below the lens, `ENEMY_ART`.

In the card pictures theme the whole picture lies over that drawing once it has loaded, with only the damage count on top; the drawing stays under it, so it still names the card and its values to assistive technology.

The face's shared rules (medallion, count fading, ornament lines, heart and brain) live in [`card_face.css`](../../apps/web/src/catalog/card_face.css), next to the parts they style.

Under it is one row of buttons ([`ActionRow`](../../apps/web/src/screens/ActionRow.tsx), for the details windows to share):

- on an investigator's turn, in one Actions group, the basic actions the rules allow them against the enemy (`BoardView.enemyActions`, a dry run of each command, so the rules stay in the engine: evade only one engaged with them, fight no unengaged aloof enemy, engage none already engaged with them):
  - fight, evade and engage as an action (`ActionButton.actions`, marked, like the turn row's actions left, with the game's action arrow, [`action.svg`](../../apps/web/src/symbols/action.svg), one per action it costs, `BoardView.actionCost`: Frozen in Fear stacks a second arrow on the round's first fight or evade, and its name says "Fight · 2 actions")
  - then fight and evade, as some cards allow, without one (`actions={0}`, marked with the game's free-ability symbol, [`free.svg`](../../apps/web/src/symbols/free.svg), or with the arrows a card adds to it, "Fight without an action · 1 extra action"); each mark has a column of its own beside the icon,
- the actions the enemy's own card offers them, such as a Parley,
- and at any time the card effects deal 1 damage, disengage and defeat.

Fight, evade and engage keep the details open and show the decisions they raise (the test, an attack of opportunity) in their place, the enemy still beside them ([decisions](play-screen-decisions.md#in-a-details-dialog)).

## Card actions

Every card screen shows the actions its card offers the same way ([`CardActions`](../../apps/web/src/screens/ActionRow.tsx) over `cardActionsOn`, the turn investigator's `BoardView.cardActions` on that card, found by the `id` each card view carries):
a "Card actions" group named like the turn row's buttons ("Resign · Parlor"), with the action arrow, or the free-ability symbol when the action is free.
Taking one closes the window first.

The turn row keeps a button for every card action besides.
