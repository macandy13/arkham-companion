# Play screen: investigators and enemies

The [investigator bar](../../apps/web/src/investigators/InvestigatorBar.tsx) shows each investigator's card, drawn like the location tiles in parchment with a dark edge: their name on a plate in a dark band with where they are (beside the name, a pentagon like the game's lead investigator token marks `BoardView.lead` and the action arrow `BoardView.turn`; on the map their pawns carry the pentagon and a glowing gilt ring), the art window (the art area of the picture of its front, `--investigator-art-*` in tokens.css; setup copies the picture onto the investigator as `Investigator.imageSrc` since a resumed game has no investigator cards), their four skills as plates with the Arkham icons, each showing the printed value with its lasting boost and the boost beside it (`InvestigatorView.boosts`, changed with steppers under the plates while adjusting, `GameActions.boostSkill`) (each opening a [skill test](../../apps/web/src/investigators/SkillTestDialog.tsx): the skill tested or two added together (`SkillTestInput.addedSkill`), difficulty, modifier and the chaos tokens drawn from the bag (a row of round tokens, a symbol token showing its game symbol over its name kept for screen readers), calculated by `BoardView.skillTest` over the engine's `skillTestSuggestion` without changing the game), their counters as round tokens along its foot, and the turn controls (start turn, actions left, icon buttons for investigate, take a resource, draw and play, a button per card action the cards offer them (`BoardView.cardActions`, "Resign · Parlor"), end) under the card, and their [threat area](../../apps/web/src/investigators/ThreatArea.tsx): a row of small cards on the top edge of their card, treacheries and engaged enemies, the enemies with their health left (`EnemyView.health`, scaled by the player count through `enemyHealth`). It shows four cards (`THREAT_CARDS_SHOWN`); more scroll by whole cards with a swipe or its arrows (CSS scroll snap).

A tap on an enemy card opens its [details](../../apps/web/src/enemies/EnemyDetails.tsx): the enemy drawn as its [card](../../apps/web/src/enemies/EnemyCard.tsx) (`BoardView.enemyStats`), our own SVG frame in the location tiles' style but laid out like the printed enemy card ([`enemyCardFrame.ts`](../../apps/web/src/enemies/enemyCardFrame.ts): the name plate, fight, health left and evade on shields, traits, keywords and victory points in our own words as HTML in `foreignObject`s so they wrap, the attack as damage and horror icons on the type line, and the art cut from the picture, `ENEMY_ART`), and under it one row of buttons ([`ActionRow`](../../apps/web/src/screens/ActionRow.tsx), for the details windows to share): on an investigator's turn the basic actions the rules allow them against the enemy (`BoardView.enemyActions`, a dry run of each command, so the rules stay in the engine: evade only one engaged with them, fight no unengaged aloof enemy, engage none already engaged with them), fight, evade and engage as an action (marked, like the turn row's actions left, with the game's action arrow, [`action.svg`](../../apps/web/src/symbols/action.svg)) and fight and evade, as some cards allow, without one, and at any time the card effects deal 1 damage, disengage and defeat.
