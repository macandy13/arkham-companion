# Campaign

A campaign is its own event-sourced aggregate in [`campaign/`](../../packages/engine/src/campaign/) with its own commands, events and reducer, so a game command can never change it. [`CampaignLog`](../../packages/engine/src/campaign/campaign_log.ts) holds the team (keyed by `InvestigatorCode`) with trauma, XP and fate, story records, chaos bag and scenario order; a game keeps a copy in `GameState.campaign`. Reached through the `campaign` namespace (`campaign.handleCommand`). First definition: [Night of the Zealot](../../packages/scenarios/src/night_of_the_zealot/campaign.ts).

A scenario's resolutions are data on `ScenarioDefinition.resolutions` ([`resolution.ts`](../../packages/engine/src/campaign/resolution.ts)): per resolution id, plus "no resolution", a list of `ResolutionStep`s (record, chaos tokens, XP from a bonus and the [victory points](../../packages/engine/src/victory.ts), trauma, killed/insane, the end of the campaign, or a step the players do by hand), plus steps that read the ended game: `when` picks its steps by a `GameCondition` (a card in play, set aside, in a scenario deck or in the victory display, the current agenda, or any of these) and `recordNames` writes the names of the cards whose condition holds as a list record. Steps name their target (`each`, `lead`, `notResigned`) instead of investigators, so `campaign.proposeResolution` reads the ended game and turns them into campaign commands, followed by the trauma of each defeat: 1 physical for damage, 1 mental for horror, a `choice` between them for both at once (`damageAndHorror`), and what a card effect names (`Investigator.defeat`, e.g. `defeatEachInvestigator({ trauma: 'physical' })`). An investigator the proposal kills, drives insane, or gives trauma up to their health or sanity on the log gets no change after that one, since the campaign rejects commands for them. It only proposes: the players confirm or edit the list, and `campaign.applyResolution` applies the confirmed commands in one step, all or none, followed by `AdvanceScenario`, which adds the scenario and its outcome to `CampaignLog.playedScenarios` and moves on. The last scenario's `endCampaign` step proposes `EndCampaign`, which sets `CampaignLog.result` (`won`, `lost`, or `survived` for an ending that is neither); a log without a result is still running. Story keys of a campaign live next to its definition ([`story.ts`](../../packages/scenarios/src/night_of_the_zealot/story.ts)), so the scenario that writes a record and the one that reads it share the key.

## Extension points

- **A new campaign:** a `CampaignDefinition` next to its scenarios.
- **A new command or record:** add it in `campaign/` with its events, reducer case and invariant.
- **A new correction:** a `CampaignAdjustment` case in `handle_campaign_adjustment.ts` that produces the regular event.
- **A new kind of resolution step:** a `ResolutionStep` case and its command in `proposeStep`; a new target is a `ResolutionTarget` case.
- **A new question about the ended game:** a `GameCondition` case in `gameHolds`, or a `CardPlace` case in `isAt`.
