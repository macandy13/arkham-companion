# Play screen: map and locations

The [map](../../apps/web/src/map/MapBoard.tsx) is an SVG of location tiles at their layout cells with pan, zoom and drag-to-play pieces (its tabs and small zoom icons float over its top corners, and its name is for screen readers only); a tap on a tile (or its "i" button, for the keyboard) opens the location's [details](../../apps/web/src/map/LocationDetails.tsx): its card (the tile's drawing with its pieces, at zoomed-card size), its shroud and clues, who is there, the cards attached to it, and an action row (the enemy details' [`ActionRow`](../../apps/web/src/screens/ActionRow.tsx)) built from a list of what the investigator whose turn it is can do there: investigate as an action or take a clue without a test at their own location, move as an action or free to a connected one (`BoardView.connectedTo`), with the corrections (reveal, clue and doom steppers) in its place while adjusting; a tap on an enemy token (a press let go where it went down, `isTap`, which a drag is not) opens the enemy's details as from the threat area.

Location tiles are drawn like the printed location card, with our own SVG frame ([`cardOrnament.tsx`](../../apps/web/src/map/cardOrnament.tsx)) over art cut from the same area of the scan, so the printed frame stays hidden under ours. Card pictures are fetched from ArkhamDB on the device and cached by the service worker, never bundled.
