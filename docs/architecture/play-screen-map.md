# Play screen: map and locations

The [map](../../apps/web/src/map/MapBoard.tsx) is an SVG of location tiles at their layout cells with pan, zoom and drag-to-play pieces (its tabs and small zoom icons float over its top corners, and its name is for screen readers only). A location without a book cell gets one from [`tileCells`](../../apps/web/src/map/mapLayout.ts): the first free card step beside a placed location it connects to (right, below, left, above), else a spare row below the map, so locations put into play without a cell still sit by their neighbours (M6.3).
The map tabs follow the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern: one Tab stop, the arrow keys, Home and End choose the map.

A tap on a tile (or its "i" button, for the keyboard) opens the location's [details](../../apps/web/src/map/LocationDetails.tsx) on the card shell (`DetailsDialog`; on a phone in the map row instead, the [location mode](play-screen.md#location-mode)):

- its card beside the page (the tile's drawing with its pieces, as the shell's `drawn` card),
- its shroud and its clues left of its clue value for the player count (`LocationView.clueValue`, "1 / 2") as the page's values, or "Unrevealed",
- who is there,
- the cards attached to it (each opening the card zoom),
- and an action row (the enemy details' [`ActionRow`](../../apps/web/src/screens/ActionRow.tsx)) with one Actions group, those that count off an action first, built from a list of what the investigator whose turn it is can do there:
  - investigate as an action or take a clue without a test at their own location,
  - move as an action or free to a connected one (`BoardView.connectedTo`), each marked with an action arrow per action it costs (`BoardView.actionCost`: Frozen in Fear raises the round's first move, a free one too),
  - where a card forbids it (`LocationView.forbidden`) investigate off and no move (Locked Door, the unrevealed Parlor's barrier), and taking a clue off while a card forbids the investigator to discover clues (`InvestigatorView.forbidden`, The Masked Hunter),
  - plus the location's own card actions (`CardActions`, the Parlor's Resign),

  While adjusting, the corrections (reveal, clue and doom steppers) take its place.

  A move closes the details; an investigation shows its test in their place, the location still beside it ([decisions](play-screen-decisions.md#in-a-details-dialog)).

The pieces on a tile stand in rows along its bottom edge ([`piecesOnTile`](../../apps/web/src/map/mapLayout.ts)): each investigator's pawn followed by the enemies engaged with them, faded since the threat area shows them in full, then after a thin line the enemies engaged with no one. Order alone shows an engagement on the map. An enemy token ([`EnemyToken`](../../apps/web/src/map/LocationTile.tsx)) shows its damage as a damage token at its lower right (`damageBadgeCentre`), the doom on it (a cultist's, `EnemyView.doom`) as a doom token at its upper left (`doomBadgeCentre`), each badge the token set's face with its count in a disc (`SvgGameToken`, MD.8b), and, when exhausted, its initial on its side. A location's doom is a doom token on its face too; the header's agenda doom is still a very dark red disc with black tentacles ([`DoomMark`](../../apps/web/src/screens/DoomMark.tsx), our own drawing; `--color-doom-token`, `--color-doom-mark`); the token pool and a dragged token (`TokenGhost`) are drawn from the token set ([`GameToken`](../../apps/web/src/tokens/GameToken.tsx), MD.8a). Pawns and enemy tokens show an avatar cut from their card's picture once it has loaded ([`TokenPicture`](../../apps/web/src/map/TokenPicture.tsx)): the investigator's face (`INVESTIGATOR_AVATAR`) and the enemy's figure (`ENEMY_AVATAR`, from `MapEnemyView.image`), squares measured on the scans in [`cardArt.ts`](../../apps/web/src/catalog/cardArt.ts) and filled into a circle inside the ring as a pattern, so the token keeps the box of its circle for taps and focus. The initial stays beneath until then, and for good when the picture fails or its scan is turned. The players may keep enemy tokens to their initials (the "Enemy tokens" setting, `Settings.enemyTokens`, set on the root as `data-enemy-tokens`): [`map.css`](../../apps/web/src/map/map.css) then hides the enemy's avatar and shows the initial, so the choice needs no prop through the map.
A tap on an enemy token (a press let go where it went down, `isTap`, which a drag is not) opens the enemy's details as from the threat area.
Tokens move by drag too (M3.11c): a clue dragged off a location's clue count (`TilePiece`'s `clue`, a pawn-sized grip around the small clue token, `data-drag='clue'`) and dropped on the pawn of an investigator at that location, or on their panel beside the map, is theirs, discovered as a card grants it (`GameActions.takeClue` with that investigator), whoever's turn it is. The panel is found by a hit test under the pointer ([`investigatorAt`](../../apps/web/src/investigators/dropTarget.ts), its `data-investigator`), since the drag keeps the pointer captured by the clue. A clue token follows the pointer over the whole page ([`TokenGhost`](../../apps/web/src/screens/TokenGhost.tsx), a portal into `body` placed by custom properties), so it stays in sight off the map, and a drop anywhere else leaves the clue where it was. A tap on the count still opens the location. The other way, a tile carries its id as `data-location`, so a clue dragged off an investigator's card is placed on the location under the pointer ([`locationAt`](../../apps/web/src/map/dropTarget.ts), [investigators](play-screen-investigators.md)).
Doom moves the same way, and both tokens come from the header's token pool ([`TokenPool`](../../apps/web/src/screens/TokenPool.tsx), a doom, a clue, a damage, a horror and a resource token in rows of three in the header's top left corner, apart from its cards, `data-pool`; a phone has no room for it; damage, horror and resources go onto cards, see [investigators](play-screen-investigators.md)). Pool doom dragged onto a location or an enemy (hit-tested by [`enemyAt`](../../apps/web/src/map/dropTarget.ts), the map token's or a threat-area card's `data-enemy`, checked before the location under it) is placed there (`adjustTokens`, `PlaceDoom`), as a card effect does, so it counts towards the threshold, and onto the agenda it is placed there (`placeDoom`); a pool clue dragged onto a location is added there (`adjustTokens`). A location's doom (`TilePiece`'s `doom`, with the same pawn-sized grip as the clue count, `Grip`) or an enemy's (`enemyDoom`, its doom badge, which keeps the pointer from the enemy token under it, so the enemy stays put; a tap on it opens the enemy) dragged onto another location, an enemy, or the agenda (hit-tested by [`overHeaderCard`](../../apps/web/src/screens/headerDropTarget.ts), the header place's `data-slot`), moves there as one step (`GameActions.moveDoom`, a `ManualAdjust` on each, since the doom in play stays the same). An enemy's damage badge sits beside its token like the doom badge, and damage dragged off it onto the pool heals one (`enemyDamage`, `GameActions.healEnemy`, `HealEnemy` by rule, as a card heals it). A location's clue or doom, or an enemy's doom, dropped on the pool (`overTokenPool`) goes back to it. A doom token follows the pointer (`TokenGhost`). The pool's tokens are no drag source while a step is queued or the players adjust the board.

## Location tiles

Location tiles are drawn like the printed location card in the Exact card theme (M3.10): our own SVG face over art cut from the same area of the scan, so the printed frame stays hidden under ours. The face is drawn in scan units (a tile is half an ArkhamDB scan, `TILE_PER_SCAN`), at the places measured on the scans (`LOCATION_FACE` in [`cardOrnament.tsx`](../../apps/web/src/map/cardOrnament.tsx)): the name plate over the frame's band with its pendant, the page arching up into the art, the set medallion with the type plate, the shroud and clue discs at the ends of the page's edge, the connection sockets along the foot. The pieces (pawns, enemies, attachments, the details button) stay in tile units on top.

The parts every card face shares live in [`cardFace.tsx`](../../apps/web/src/catalog/cardFace.tsx), also in scan units: the `CountCircle`, the count's token (`SvgGameToken`) on a printed value's lower right with the current count in its disc (here the clues left on the printed clue value, `LocationView.printedClues`; later the doom on the agenda's threshold, the damage on an enemy's health), the `SetMedallion` with its type plate and the card's encounter set icon, and the rounded card outline. The set icon is the view's `encounterSet` (from `CardStats.encounterCode`, on locations, enemies and encounter cards): a span whose CSS mask is that set's SVG, cut from the symbol sheets into [`symbols/sets/`](../../apps/web/src/symbols/sets/) and mapped by [`sets.css`](../../apps/web/src/symbols/sets.css), so it takes the medallion's ring colour in every theme. Sets are added campaign by campaign; a card without one keeps the plain medallion. Their colours are the `--color-face-*` tokens, so a card theme recolours them in the stylesheet.
A drawn card theme other than Exact is only a set of these tokens under `:root[data-card-theme='…']` in [`tokens.css`](../../apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css) (`data-card-theme` is `Settings.cardTheme`, set by `applySettings`): Night and brass and Eldritch set the faces' tokens there, and the paper and ink tokens on the investigator card alone, since dialogs share them. Eldritch also swaps the ornament's style: every `OrnamentLine` carries a tapered tentacle along its line (`tapered`), which only that theme's stylesheet shows, in place of the three strokes. In the same way `SetMedallion` carries an eye that replaces its inner ring when the card has no set icon, and each face lays a `NightSky` over its page: stars spread by a fixed sequence (`skyStars`), clipped to the page's outline, under the text; the page's gradient is the vignette. Both show in Eldritch alone. A card's name takes the `--font-family-card-title` token, the display face unless a theme sets another; Eldritch sets the blackletter Grenze Gotisch. The count, damage, horror, skill and chaos token colours stay the same in every theme.

The location's connection symbol (`LocationView.symbol`) sits in the tile's top left corner where the card prints it, a disc in the symbol's printed colour (`--color-connection-*` tokens) with our own glyph ([`connectionSymbol.ts`](../../apps/web/src/map/connectionSymbol.ts)); the details button mirrors it in the top right corner. The view gives the symbol of the side the card shows: a location whose unrevealed side prints another one (`CardStats.unrevealedSymbol`, every Arkham Woods' square) shows that one until it is revealed. Fourteen symbols are drawn: Night of the Zealot's ten, and the four the Core Set (2026) prints (heart, star, spade, clover), added together before all of its scenarios are in; comma joins `LocationSymbol` with the first scenario that needs it ([location-symbols.md](../location-symbols.md)).

A revealed location's text box prints its action abilities, whoever may take them (`LocationView.printedActions`: the action or free symbol and the name, "Resign."), as HTML in a `foreignObject` styled like the enemy card's text; with the whole card picture shown (the "Card pictures" card theme, `Settings.cardTheme`) it gives way to the printed text, and so do the face's frame, name, shroud and clue value, while the clues left stay on the printed clue value.

Card pictures are fetched from ArkhamDB on the device and cached by the service worker, never bundled.
