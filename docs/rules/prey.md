# Prey

[Prey](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Prey): used to pick between several investigators when the enemy auto-engages or when a hunter has equidistant targets.
Ties go to the lead investigator.
"Only" in the prey text makes the enemy ignore everyone else for moving and engaging, though others can still engage it with an action or a card.

- "Highest [combat]" or "Lowest [agility]" compares the investigators' skill values (Ghoul Priest, Young Deep One, Relentless Dark Young). The app reads the printed skill plus its lasting boost, which the players set for player cards in play (Beat Cop's +1 [combat]); a modifier that lasts only one test does not count.
- "Most / Least remaining health or sanity", "Most damage / horror / clues / resources" compare the investigators' counters ("least" and "fewest" mean the same as "lowest").
- "Nearest to another [[Cultist]] enemy" (Narôgath): the investigator with the fewest connections to the nearest other enemy with that trait; with none on the table everyone ties.
- Prey the app can't read from the board (Yithian Observer's "Fewest cards in hand", as the app doesn't track hands): the lead investigator picks the investigator who fits it, asked as "Whom does the prey pick?"; a tie among those is the lead's pick too.
