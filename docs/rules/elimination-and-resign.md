# Elimination and resign

[Elimination](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Elimination), [resign](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Resign).

- Defeat or resigning eliminates the investigator. Resigning is not a defeat.
- Lita Chantler (The Gathering's Parlor): while no player controls her, she gains "[action]: Parley. Test [intellect] (4). If you succeed, take control of Lita Chantler." Only investigators at her location can take it, and as a Parley it provokes no attacks of opportunity. The investigator who succeeds controls her (she is in play in their play area); her own Ally abilities are then theirs to use, played by hand.
- Umôrdhoth (The Devourer Below): "[action] If you control Lita Chantler: … (→R3)". Investigators at its location can take it. It is not a Parley, so it provokes attacks of opportunity, Umôrdhoth's own included (massive, it is engaged with everyone at its location). In The Devourer Below Lita is a player card the app doesn't track, so it trusts the investigator who takes the action to control her, but only when someone earned her in The Gathering (Campaign Guide: no resolution, R1, and R3 earn her; R2 doesn't). Earning her only lets the investigator include her in their deck, so even then the app trusts the taker. In a campaign where nobody earned her, nobody can control her and the action is not offered. A standalone game has no log: its players pick the earlier resolutions themselves (Rules Reference, "Standalone Mode"), so the app trusts the taker there too.
- Resign is an action ability a card offers ("[action] Resign."): the Parlor, the Main Path and The Midnight Masks' agendas. There is no other way to resign. Like Parley it provokes no attacks of opportunity.
- On elimination: "when the game ends" effects on their weaknesses in play trigger, then those weaknesses are removed. Cards they control and all their out-of-play cards are removed from the game. Cards they own but don't control stay until they leave play, and are then removed. Their clues go to their location, and their resources return to the pool. Engaged enemies are placed unengaged at that location and otherwise keep their state. Other cards in their threat area go to the appropriate discard pile.
- A new lead investigator is chosen if needed, from those still in the scenario. One effect that eliminates several investigators ("each investigator … is defeated") eliminates them together ([simultaneous resolution](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Priority_of_Simultaneous_Resolution)), so the new lead comes from those left afterwards. If nobody is left, the scenario ends with "no resolution", and the players read the scenario's "if no resolution was reached" entry in the campaign guide (Learn to Play).
- A card ability can defeat an investigator outright; what follows from that is only what the ability says ([investigator defeat](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Investigator_Defeat)).
- Eliminated investigators still count for per-investigator values.
