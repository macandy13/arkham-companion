# Card abilities and modifiers

[Constant abilities](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Constant_Abilities), [in play and out of play](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#In_Play_and_Out_of_Play), [modifiers](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Modifiers); wording from [The Arkham Grimoire v1.1](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/4e/da/4eda7782-c983-47cc-8d9f-ae372a44d87b/arkham_grimoire_v11.pdf).

- A constant ability applies only while its card is in play. Set-aside, removed, in-deck, in-hand, discarded and victory-display cards are out of play, so their abilities do nothing. An attachment is in play while its host is.
- An unrevealed location has only its unrevealed side's text. Its revealed side's abilities ("each enemy at this location gets +1 fight") apply once it is revealed. This is our reading of "do not read the revealed side" (Grimoire, Location Cards); no rule says it in one sentence.
- A modified value is the printed value plus all active modifiers, applied together and recalculated whenever one starts or stops. A result below zero counts as zero.
- An engaged enemy is at its investigator's location, so "each enemy at X" includes enemies engaged with investigators at X.
- An [action ability](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Abilities) ("[action]: …") on an encounter or scenario card is taken as an action in the investigator's own turn. Who may take it depends on where the card is: a location's by investigators at that location, an enemy's by investigators at its location (engaged with it or not), a card's in a threat area by investigators at that investigator's location, and the current agenda's and act's by any investigator. An unrevealed location offers none, as it has only its unrevealed side's text.
- [Limits](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Limits_and_Maximums): "(Limit once per turn/game.)" counts each investigator's uses apart, "(Group limit once per game.)" everyone's together. The engine counts "once per turn" by round, since card actions are taken only in the investigator's own turn and each investigator has one turn per round. An action whose effect can't change the game state can't be taken ([Triggered Abilities](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Triggered_Abilities)); the engine still offers it for now (#455).
- [Costs](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Costs): the text before the colon ("Spend 5 resources:") is paid in full before the effect; an action whose cost can't be paid can't be taken. "The investigators spend 2 clues per investigator, as a group" (Uncovering the Conspiracy) is paid by any investigators together, wherever they are, splitting it as they choose, like advancing the act ([Act Deck and Agenda Deck](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Act_Deck_and_Agenda_Deck)); "per investigator" counts the investigators who started the scenario. For now the app doesn't ask who pays: the investigator taking the action pays what they can and the others the rest, in seat order (#488). "Choose and discard 4 cards from your hand" (Herman Collins' Parley) is paid by the players, since the game holds no player cards; the app trusts that they can pay it. Attacks of opportunity come after the costs are paid; the engine pays a card action's cost after them for now. Only Uncovering the Conspiracy notices: if an attack of opportunity defeats the investigator taking it, their clues drop to their location before the cost is paid, so the group may no longer afford it (the rules would have spent those clues first).
- "You must either A or B" (and "the lead investigator must decide") offers only the options that can change the game state ([Must](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Must)): with no clues, "spend 1 clue or take 2 damage" leaves only the damage. With neither possible, nothing happens ([Effects](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Effects): resolve as much as you are able). An option that resolves only in part is still allowed by the rule; the engine drops it for now (no Night of the Zealot card has one).
