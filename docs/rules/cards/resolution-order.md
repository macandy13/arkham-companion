# Resolution order

Card rulings for [Damage, horror and defeat](../damage-horror-and-defeat.md) and [Skill tests](../skill-tests.md).
Each part of a card resolves in the order its text gives, and "when" abilities resolve before what triggers them ([Timing of forced effects](../timing-of-forced-effects.md)).
The cards below need a stand-in for that order in the engine, or a ruling on what follows a test.

- "When [this enemy] attacks: heal 1 damage from him" ("Wolf-Man" Drew) resolves after the attack in the engine, a stand-in for "when".
  - It only matters when a player card damages him during his attack (Guard Dog), which the players resolve by hand, healing him first.
  - Besides that, only a skipped attack of opportunity (a cancelled attack, whose "when" abilities would still resolve) heals him in the rules and not in the engine.
- On Wings of Darkness's "Then, disengage with each non-[[Nightgaunt]] enemy engaged with you and move to a [[Central]] location" follows the test whether it passed or failed, and does nothing for a drawer the fail defeated (see Skill tests, "Results that defeat the tester").
  - The drawer picks the Central location when there are several (our reading of "you … move"; the RR's "Choices" gives open choices to the lead).
  - Their own location is not one to move to ([Move](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Move): a move to the current location can't happen), so they stay when it is the only one.
  - A disengaged enemy is ready and unengaged, so once the drawer has moved it engages whoever is still at its location, the drawer included when they stayed ([Enemy Engagement](https://arkhamdb.com/rules#Enemy_Engagement): "any time a ready unengaged enemy is at the same location as an investigator"; checked after the whole sentence, our reading).
