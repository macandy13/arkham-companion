# Story assets and earned cards

Card rulings for [Elimination and resign](../elimination-and-resign.md).
A story asset is an encounter card the investigators can take control of; a resolution can let an investigator earn it for their deck.
The app tracks it while it is an encounter card and only trusts the players once it becomes a player card.

- Lita Chantler (The Gathering's Parlor): while no player controls her, she gains "[action]: Parley. Test [intellect] (4). If you succeed, take control of Lita Chantler."
  - Only investigators at her location can take it, and as a Parley it provokes no attacks of opportunity.
  - The investigator who succeeds controls her (she is in play in their play area); her own Ally abilities are then theirs to use, played by hand.
- Umôrdhoth (The Devourer Below): "[action] If you control Lita Chantler: … (→R3)". Investigators at its location can take it.
  - It is not a Parley, so it provokes attacks of opportunity, Umôrdhoth's own included (massive, it is engaged with everyone at its location).
  - In The Devourer Below Lita is a player card the app doesn't track, so it trusts the investigator who takes the action to control her, but only when that investigator earned her in The Gathering (Campaign Guide: no resolution and R1 give her to the lead; R3 to an investigator the players choose; R2 to nobody).
  - Earning her only lets the investigator include her in their deck, so even then the app trusts the taker.
  - An investigator who didn't earn her can't control her, so the action is not offered to them.
  - Not handled yet: when nobody resigned, R3 lets the players choose one of the new investigators to earn her (Campaign Guide: "choose an investigator to earn the Lita Chantler card"), by hand, and the log can't record that, so the action stays hidden from them (#625).
  - A standalone game has no log: its players pick the earlier resolutions themselves (Rules Reference, "Standalone Mode"), so the app trusts the taker there too.
