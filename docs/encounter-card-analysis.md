# Encounter card analysis

What the scenario cards ask of the engine beyond the generic rules in [rules.md](rules.md), and the plan to support it. Written from every Chapter Two encounter card, read one by one, plus a pattern survey of all Chapter One encounter cards. Plans here are not built yet; the PR that builds one describes it in [architecture.md](architecture.md) and ticks it off here.

## Source and method

- **Data:** [arkhamdb-json-data](https://github.com/Kamalisk/arkhamdb-json-data) at commit `6f4760e` (2026-10-14), the repository ArkhamDB itself is built from. `pnpm carddata:fetch --json-data <checkout>` normalises it into `packages/carddata/.cache/` (gitignored, like every fetched card).
- **Chapter Two, read card by card:** all 211 encounter cards of the two products marked `chapter: 2` in `packs.json`: the Core Set (2026) (`core_2026`, 92 cards, scenarios _Spreading Flames_, _Smoke and Mirrors_, _Queen of Ash_) and Children of Blood (`cob`, 119 cards, _River of Blood_, _New Horizons_, _Blood Money_). Rules for new Children of Blood mechanics come from its [campaign guide](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/a8/9c/a89c98b9-9de4-4b6c-8110-80fedbdcdb60/ahc106_campaign_guide-web.pdf).
- **Chapter One, surveyed:** 3,853 encounter cards from 145 scenarios, counted by regular expressions over the rules text (keywords, trigger windows, effect phrases). The counts rank how often a mechanic recurs; they are not exact.
- **Not in the data:** location connection symbols, scenario setup, codex entries and resolutions. These live on the card images and in the campaign guides (see "Data gaps").

## At a glance

1. **Most of the work is generic.** About two dozen generic primitives (below) cover nearly every Chapter Two card. The truly one-off effects are mostly agenda and act reverse sides, which are scenario hooks already.
2. **Three new keywords:** _Doomed_ (core), _Predator_ and wider use of _Elusive_ (Children of Blood). The engine parses none of them yet.
3. **Skill tests need the margin.** 11 Chapter Two cards (and 125 Chapter One cards) scale with "for each point you fail by" or "succeed by 2 or more", but a skill test answer is only pass or fail.
4. **Locations carry state.** Attachments that change a location (Fire!, Arcane Lock, Flash Flood), extra costs to enter or leave, connections defined in text, and location _versions_ chosen by difficulty or time of day.
5. **Players' cards stay physical, so many effects are reminders.** Many forced effects hit hands, decks or assets the app does not track. A generic "timing window reminder" covers them all without card scripts.
6. **Children of Blood adds a chaos bag economy:** blood tokens added to the bag and sealed on investigators (max 3 each, 12 in total), read by prey, test difficulty and the reference card.
7. **Data gap in code:** `CHAPTER_TWO_PACK_CODES` lists only `core_2026`; `cob` is Chapter Two too, and the data carries a `chapter` field to read instead.

## Support tiers

Each special case is put in one tier. The tier decides how much code a card needs.

| Tier         | Meaning                                                                     | When                                                                                                                          |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Automate** | The engine applies the effect.                                              | It only touches state the app tracks: doom, clues, enemies, locations, encounter deck, damage and horror, actions, resources. |
| **Assist**   | The engine raises a decision with prefilled options and applies the answer. | The effect needs a choice or a test result, but its outcome is tracked state.                                                 |
| **Remind**   | The app shows the card text at the right moment; players resolve it.        | It hits hands, decks, assets or other player cards (out of scope, see [product.md](product.md)).                              |

The engine has `turnEndTest` and `discardAtRoundEnd` as card data already. Generalising these into a **timing window** list (enter location, end turn at location, turn begins, discover clues, failed test at location, phase ends, round ends, enemy engages, treachery resolved) gives one place to hang both hooks and reminders.

## Data gaps

- **Connections:** no ArkhamDB field; planned in M4.2 (symbol extraction) and M4.3 (editor).
- **Setup and codex:** campaign guide only; M4.1 imports ArkhamCards campaign data for setup. Codex texts stay with the players' book; the app names the entry.
- **Chapter of a pack:** `packs.json` has `chapter`; the code hard-codes `CHAPTER_TWO_PACK_CODES = ['core_2026']`, which misses `cob`.
- **Location versions:** same-named cards (Downtown, Sewer Tunnels, Dawn/Dusk) differ only by subname or trait; setup must name the code, not the name.
- **Reprints:** the data repository stores reprints as `duplicate_of` stubs without name or text; the importer copies the original's fields.

## Implementation plan

Ordered so each step unlocks the most cards for the scenarios coming next (M4 _Spreading Flames_, M5 the rest of the Core Set, M7 Children of Blood). Each line is one task of one or a few small PRs; the task files get them when their milestone starts.

### Before M4 (data and parsing)

1. **Card data from the data repository** – `pnpm carddata:fetch --json-data <dir>` (this analysis, PR alongside).
2. **Chapter from the data** – read `chapter` from `packs.json`, drop the hard-coded list.
3. **New keywords** – parse Elusive, Doomed, Predator; Patrol and Prey details with enemy and trait targets; "+N per investigator" health into `ScaledValue`.

### M4 – Spreading Flames, and the generic rules it needs

4. **Doomed and Elusive** – rules with unit tests and an end-to-end step (Bystander in _Spreading Flames_).
5. **Scenario actions** – cost (actions, resources, clues as a group per investigator), condition, test, effect, ability limits; Parley and Resign on agendas, acts and locations. Replaces the one-off Parley in M4.5.
6. **Skill test margin** – the answer carries "by how much"; the suggestion computes it; revelation tests use it.
7. **Location attachments and modifiers** – Fire! (attach to nearest without Fire!, phase-end damage), shroud and movement costs from attachments.
8. **Timing windows** – one list of windows, used by hooks and by reminders; migrate `turnEndTest` and `discardAtRoundEnd`.

### M5 – Smoke and Mirrors, Queen of Ash

9. **Cards beneath cards** – zone `beneath` (locations, act); draw facedown card; count Elite under the act.
10. **Codex reminders** – card ability or defeat names a codex entry; the app shows the number and lets the players apply the outcome commands.
11. **Text connections and movement costs** – Sewer Culvert, entering Underground Cistern and Sluice Control.
12. **Replacement hooks and merged act/agenda** – Brethren of Ash.
13. **Patrol movement** and enemy restrictions (cannot move, no attacks of opportunity, cannot be damaged).

### M7 – Children of Blood

14. **Blood tokens** – chaos bag counts in the campaign log, sealed tokens per investigator, limits; prey and difficulty read them.
15. **Predator** – enemy phase step between hunters and attacks.
16. **Location versions** – setup picks Dawn/Dusk, Day/Night and random versions.
17. **Scenario records and snares** – "remember that …" conditions, Manager's Office entry rule, Julia Stern's snares.
18. **Act deck reset, reference-card tokens, enemy vs enemy damage** – the remaining River of Blood and Blood Money hooks.

Everything in the _remind_ tier rides on step 8 and needs no per-card code.
